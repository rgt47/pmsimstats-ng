# Cover letter
*2026-10-28 17:40 PDT*

To the Editor,
Statistics in Medicine

Dear Editor:

We submit a manuscript on the power of the Hybrid N-of-1 design of
Hendrickson and colleagues (2020) for the treatment main effect. The
Hybrid combines open-label titration, blinded discontinuation and a
brief crossover.

The study simulates the Hybrid and three comparators, an alternating
N-of-1 design, a two-period crossover and a parallel-group trial,
on a common estimand, the mean drug response on drug. It varies
effect size, carryover half-life and decay form, treatment
heterogeneity, within-participant variability, the number of periods,
sample size, serial correlation, period length and biomarker
correlation. The analysis half-life is crossed with the
data-generating half-life, so the cost of unmodeled carryover is
separate from the cost of carryover. Covariance matrices that are
not positive definite are dropped, not repaired. We report bias,
standard-error calibration, coverage and Monte Carlo standard errors
for every cell.

The manuscript is original, has not been published elsewhere and is
not under consideration by another journal. The study is exploratory.
No analysis plan was registered in advance, and the findings should
be confirmed in an independent study. The work received no specific
funding, and the authors declare no conflicts of interest. The code
and stored simulation output are in the project repository, and a
public address will be added at submission.
