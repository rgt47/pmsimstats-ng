## 5. Observation outside the manuscript text

The G10 mean estimates in `fig4b-g10-cbm0.25.csv` at $N = 70$ without
censoring are -2.14 (Hybrid), -2.39 (OL+BDC) and -1.94 (crossover) per
SD of biomarker at $t_{1/2} = 0$, against $-c_{bm}\sigma_{BR} = -2.0$.

not computed). The paper reports only G10 power, so this is not a
manuscript error, but it bears on calling G10 the recommended analysis
and may reflect the half-life grid of I7. Worth a check in paper 02.
